## Players and training

* Direct players reuse every incumbent tensor and add a scalar times the change in CROSS mobility to each candidate logit. The tested weights are 0, 0.25, 1 and 4. Mobility is the log of one plus the legal count clipped at 16, divided by the log of 17. Zero is the original checkpoint. Direct parameter search has been evaluated in conventional Tetris. The game geometry and results here are specific to this engine. [Scherrer et al.](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v16/scherrer15a.html)
* A learned variant freezes the original actor and trains a 2,241-parameter residual head with the 64 candidate hidden activations, two mobility values, visible preview and observed bag flags. Its zero final layer preserves the starting logits. Bounding each correction to ±0.05 protects any original top-two margin greater than 0.10. The critic still learns.
* The lookahead player uses the visible next piece. If the greedy move has at most eight remaining CROSS origins, it examines up to 16 actions and four teacher-ranked replies. If the preview already has no legal landing, it first searches the full shortlist for a rescue. Within that frontier it adds the best reply mobility to the current actor logit. Public information determines the decision.
* PPO compares full actor at temperature 1, full actor at 0.25, temperature 0.25 with a 0.05 frozen-incumbent KL penalty, and temperature 0.25 with the bounded residual. All four keep the same placement reward. Temperature applies to the sampled behavior, likelihood ratios, entropy and KL. The choice policy used in evaluation is greedy. [Schulman et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06347) · [Policy regularization and distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.03835)

### Physical rescue at deployment

The optional rescue-only player keeps the highest-scoring move whenever the visible next piece has a legal landing on the resulting board. If that move would end the game, it takes the highest-scoring shortlisted move that leaves a landing, with the usual actor tie order. If every move loses immediately, it keeps the original choice. The rule examines only public game information. It needs neither new network weights nor teacher reply scoring. This is an application of correcting an actor's proposed move against an explicit constraint. [Alshiekh et al., action shielding](https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.08611)

For a deterministic base player and the same piece stream, rescue-only plays the identical sequence until the first override. At that placement the base would commit its move and physically top out. Rescue-only also commits a legal move, so its placement count is already at least the base count. It gains another placement if its controller can play the next piece. This argument applies to placements measured through physical top-out with matched numerical and tie settings. Equal wall-time throughput and a positive average gain require separate measurements.

Desktop playback accepts `--neural-checkpoint PATH --neural-rescue-only`. The evaluator accepts `--rescue-only` with `--ppo` and uses a distinct cache identity for completed games and partial resumes. The proactive lookahead switches still select the registered 16-action, four-reply player. The running experiment keeps its preregistered roster.

Each rescue-only game now also reports the matched greedy lifetime, stop reason and whether the greedy move physically topped out. The evaluator records the first placement that greedy would lose and carries that count through interruptions. Both players share every preceding decision, so a second baseline rollout is unnecessary under the same inference settings. In the seed 300122 regression an independent greedy replay physically topped out at absolute placement 21,517. The one-pass evaluator recovered that count both uninterrupted and after resuming on the decisive move. When the measurement cap was exactly 21,517 it reported both the cap and the physical loss. ### Preserving the last CROSS landing

If the board is already at zero, or no retained move can preserve a landing, it plays greedy. The test uses only the current board and candidates and can fire again later. Desktop playback accepts `--neural-preserve-last-cross` and the PPO evaluator accepts `--preserve-last-cross`. With mobility features on the GPU, selection uses a batched masked argmax and transfers only the chosen move and requested counters. This earlier intervention can change the trajectory before greedy would have died. Its survival effect needs matched games.

### Can the actor tell when CROSS is coming?

In 652,639 placements from 64 archived training streams, legal CROSS origins fell from positive to zero 67 times. A layer clear reopened a landing in only three cases, after 10, 11 and 16 placements counting the move that consumed the last landing. The other 64 intervals ended on CROSS. A placement without a clear can only add occupied cells. If no layer clear restores a landing, the number of placements from the first loss to top-out is bounded by when the next CROSS must arrive. These frequencies describe games selected for eventual physical top-out. [Full-game reconstruction and recovery cases](../validation/cross-survival-public-bag.json)

The expanded game shuffles one of each of 15 pieces per bag. The current and visible pieces, the number placed, and the observed CROSS count determine an exact distribution for the distance to the next CROSS. We checked that distribution by independently enumerating all 3,375 combinations of bag phase and CROSS location in two bags. Across 15,360 fixed opening positions, the eight-placement arrival forecast had Brier error 0.1395 versus 0.1900 for an optimal predictor using only the residual head's two explicit CROSS flags. The full forecast was more accurate on all 64 streams. We then scaled the direct mobility coefficient with that forecast while keeping its unconditional mean at four. On six separately frozen roots disjoint from the timing study, one gained 26 placements, one lost seven and four tied through the 1,024 cap. On the two overlapping roots the changes were zero and −16. This is evidence for using bag phase as a forecast feature. The direct weight experiment did not establish a consistent survival gain. ## What colder PPO changed

On training seed 611000, both endpoints collected 655,360 placements. Lowering temperature from 1 to 0.25 reduced mean behavior entropy from 3.449 to 2.241 nats. The fraction of likelihood ratios outside the PPO clip band rose from 2.95% to 7.67%. Plain PPO saw 2 fresh top-outs and 10 from archived starts. Cold PPO saw 0 fresh and 10 archived top-outs, even though the latter starts contributed 40,560 placements versus 22,353 for plain. PPO scales the prior and learned logits together in both sampling and the update. Comparing the trained actors on the same 40 boards at temperature 1 gave the plain actor's greedy move a mean probability of 20.70%, versus 17.43% for the cold actor. At temperature 0.25 these rose to 53.61% and 47.36%. Changing only scoring temperature preserved every greedy move. Fresh, uncapped gameplay determines whether the learned changes help survival. [Matched temperatures and checkpoint evidence](../validation/cross-survival-cold-training.json) · [Schulman et al., PPO objective](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06347)

The two actors moved similar distances from their starting weights in relative L2 norm (3.14% plain, 3.01% cold). The final ranking output moved 2.65% for plain and 9.82% for cold, while the candidate branch moved 3.37% and 2.59% respectively. The two endpoints made different greedy moves on 18 of the 40 boards. Plain changed 11 of the 15 decisions where the incumbent's margin was at most 0.10, and cold changed five. Each changed six of the remaining 25. The frozen actor with a bounded ±0.05 residual can only overturn an original margin of at most 0.10 before rounding. The smallest of those 25 larger margins was 0.1052 and all passed the FP32 rounding check. On a predetermined sample of one board per stream, plain changed four of five near ties and cold changed one of five. These ten streams provide a small diagnostic set. [Endpoint weights, margin strata and controls](../validation/cross-survival-cold-training.json)
